Request for Proposal (RFP)
Sales Force Automation (SFA) Solution

1. Introduction
We are seeking proposals for a Sales Force Automation solution to digitize field sales execution, customer account management, order capture, inventory visibility, promotional execution, visit reporting, follow-up tracking, and management dashboards.
The preferred solution must support a mobile-first sales team operating across GCC and international markets, integrate with Sage X3 ERP, and support forecasting visibility either from Sage / Netstock or from the SFA platform where appropriate.
2. Business Objectives
•	Improve field sales discipline through digital visit planning, geo-fenced check-in/check-out, mandatory call closure, and automated follow-up reminders.
•	Provide salespeople with customer intelligence including purchase history, missed purchases, cross-sell, upsell, alternative products, and stock-driven recommendations.
•	Enable faster customer engagement through instant access to product specifications, recipes, packaging details, quotations, offers, and contract documents.
•	Automate management visibility through dashboards for sales activity, daily reports, follow-ups, pipeline, customer trends, product performance, territory performance, and exceptions.
•	Reduce manual emails, spreadsheets by integrating SFA workflows with Sage X3 & relevant communication channels.
3. Scope of Work
The selected vendor shall provide software, configuration, implementation, integration, testing, data migration support, training, rollout support, and post-go-live hypercare for the SFA solution.
Scope Area	Expected Coverage
SFA Platform	Mobile and web application for sales users and managers.
Account & Lead Management	Existing customer management, new prospect, lead qualification & conversion tracking.
Visit & Activity Management	Route / visit planning, check-in/check-out, geo-fencing, mandatory visit notes, follow-ups, and manager alerts.
Order & Inventory Flow	Order capture, Sage integration, product availability visibility, alternatives, non-moving, near-expiry, and promotion items.
Pricing & Promotions	Customer/product pricing, trade promotions, special pricing workflows, and approval routing.
AI / Automation	Voice note capture, transcription, visit summaries, action items, cross-sell / upsell suggestions, and customer risk flags.
Reporting	Dashboards and scheduled reports by salesperson, territory, customer, product category, pipeline, and follow-up status.
Integrations	Sage X3, Netstock or Sage forecasting source, email, WhatsApp, and document repositories as applicable.
Training & Change Management	Role-based training for sales users, managers, administrators, and IT support team.

4. Functional Requirements
ID	Requirement Area	Requirement Description	Priority

FR-07	Voice-enabled notes	Allow salesperson to dictate visit notes in English and Arabic where possible, convert to text, and generate summary/action items subject to customer consent where recording is used.	Must Have
FR-08	Follow-up alerts	Auto-create reminders for customer commitments and incomplete call reports; escalate overdue items to line managers.	Must Have
FR-09	Customer purchase risk flags	Flag customers who stop buying or reduce consumption of specific products versus historical pattern.	Must Have
FR-10	Cross-sell and upsell	Recommend related products based on customer type, buying history, similar customers, product category, and business strategy.	Must Have
FR-11	Product alternatives	Show top alternative products where primary product is unavailable or not suitable.	Must Have
FR-12	Inventory visibility	Show active items, non-moving products, promotion items, near-expiry items, and availability status without exposing unnecessary sensitive stock details.	Must Have
FR-13	Order capture	Capture customer orders in SFA and pass confirmed orders to Sage X3 based on approved integration design.	Must Have
FR-14	Pricing	Display customer and product pricing from Sage X3; support pricing by customer and product.	Must Have
FR-15	Special pricing approval	Allow salespeople to request special prices, discounts, or trade offers, with approval workflow and audit trail.	Must Have
FR-16	Head-office promotions	Allow head office to push active promotions, focus products, slow-moving items, and daily/weekly sales priorities to sales users.	Must Have
FR-17	Product knowledge base	Provide access to product specifications, recipes, packaging, catalogue content, and technical documents.	Must Have
FR-18	Document sharing	Share approved product details, recipes, quotations, and contract information through email and WhatsApp, subject to agreed communication model.	Must Have
FR-19	Contract management	Create, send, store, and track signed customer agreements; generate draft standard contracts from approved templates.	Must Have
FR-20	Dashboards	Provide dashboards for sales performance, pipeline, customer activity, follow-ups, visit compliance, product category, customer-wise and territory-wise reporting.	Must Have
FR-21	Scheduled reports	Send automated daily, weekly, and monthly reports to sales management.	Must Have
FR-22	Forecast visibility	Show forecast information from Sage/Netstock if available; avoid duplicate forecasting logic unless clearly justified.	Must Have
FR-23	Mobile offline capability	Support offline or low-connectivity usage for field sales, with secure sync when connectivity returns.	Must Have

6. Non-Functional Requirements
Area	Requirement
Security	Role-based access control, segregation by territory/country where needed, audit trail for pricing/approval changes, and secure mobile access.
Compliance	Recording or voice transcription must follow consent and applicable privacy requirements. Vendor must describe compliance controls.
Performance	Mobile app should respond quickly in field conditions and support low-bandwidth usage.
Scalability	Support growth beyond 35 users, additional countries, more customers, and additional product lines.
Multi-country / currency	Support GCC and international sales structures, multiple currencies, and territory-wise reporting.
Data quality	Vendor must support data mapping, validation, duplicate checks, and master-data governance recommendations.
Administration	Internal IT/admin team should be able to manage users, workflows, reports, approvals, and configuration changes.
Auditability	Track changes to customer, pricing requests, approvals, visit reports, contracts, and order submissions.
